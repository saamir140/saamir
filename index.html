GOOD TO KNOW BEFORE WE START

Questions, answered.

Understand the scope, timelines and working model before making a decision.

Can you build an e-commerce business from scratch?+

Yes. Depending on the agreed scope, this can include business and product research, brand direction, Shopify or custom-coded store development, product pages, essential setup and launch preparation.

Can I hire you only for ongoing management?+

Yes. Business build and ongoing management are separate services. If you already have an operating business, we can first understand its current position and discuss an appropriate management scope.

How does the 30-day management service work?+

Each period runs for 30 days and includes agreed strategy, planning, execution, tracking, testing and review. At the end of the period, continuing requires renewal and payment for the next 30 days.

Is ongoing management included in the business build price?+

No. The initial business build is a separate project. Ongoing management is optional and agreed separately in 30-day cycles.

Do you guarantee sales, revenue or a specific result?+

No. E-commerce outcomes depend on product-market fit, pricing, competition, demand, budget, fulfilment and other factors. The work is focused on agreed strategy, execution, measurement and continuous learning — not guaranteed revenue.

Do you work only with Shopify stores?+

Shopify is one option. Depending on requirements, a store can also be developed using custom code. The platform and scope are decided before the project begins.

How much does it cost and how long does a build take?+

Pricing and delivery time depend on the business model, platform, number of products, integrations and deliverables. These are confirmed after understanding the project — no arbitrary one-size-fits-all quote.
