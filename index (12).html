Is business setup a one-time service?+

Yes. The build is a separate project with its own agreed scope, price and delivery timeline.

How does 30-day management work?+

Each paid cycle covers the agreed strategy, planning, execution, tracking, testing and review. Renew and pay for the next cycle if you want to continue.

Are ad spend and third-party costs included?+

Not automatically. Advertising budgets, paid apps, subscriptions and other external costs are identified and agreed separately before work begins.

Can you guarantee sales or revenue?+

No. Sales depend on factors beyond service delivery, including product, demand, pricing, competition, budget and fulfilment. The commitment is to the agreed work and transparent review.
