# Competitor research for Samir’s growth website

Reviewed the five public service pages identified in section 20 of the supplied PRD on 27 November 2026. The review covered each page’s published copy, sequence, offer, evidence and next action. Public pages establish what competitors communicate. Their conversion rates and client results were outside the available evidence.

| Reference | Observed on the live page | Decision for Samir’s site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ian Rhodes, fractional ecommerce leadership](https://ecommercegrowth.com/fractional/) | Opens the offer around a team that runs individual channels but lacks an owner for the whole store. Explains how acquisition, conversion and retention connect and describes an initial workshop. | Speak to a recognizable founder problem first. Explain which decisions Samir owns, the evidence he needs and how the founder participates. |
| [Scotty Rill, services](https://build-scale-win.com/services) | Shows duration, best fit and included work for each engagement. The project and audit option has a 4–8 week window with named outputs including channel audits and roadmaps. | Give each of Samir’s four sprint phases a date range and tangible output. Present ongoing support as a separate decision after handoff. |
| [Barron Zuo, fractional ecommerce growth lead](https://barronzuo.com/services/fractional-ecommerce-growth-lead/) | A concise mandate ties channels to one reporting plan. Result cards link to named case sources and pair figures with the work and period where available. | Make the six-step method legible across channels. Require approval, evidence, metric definition and period before publishing a numeric outcome. |
| [Waqas Qureshi, pricing](https://connectwaqas.com/pricing/) | Places a fit check before pricing and explains that advertisers pay platforms from their own accounts. Lists audit outputs and the service fee separately. | State who should apply, how the engagement is scoped, and which media, production and tool costs the client covers. Use the PRD’s custom-quote policy. |
| [EcomGrowth Agency, homepage](https://www.ecomgrowthagency.com/) | Repeats an audit action at navigation, hero and closing form. The form asks for the website and monthly revenue, following a sequence of capabilities, process, results and FAQs. | Route primary actions to one application and explain what happens after submission. Keep commercial ranges optional and distinguish the free application from audit scope. |

The V3 addendum governs the design decisions where earlier sections differ. It specifies one 45-day engagement, four phases and up to three approved featured cases. The PRD’s navy and teal tokens, responsive grid and spacing set the visual direction. Competitor testimonials, price points, time commitments and numerical claims belong to their respective businesses.
